## 1. WORLD OF PART I — URASTAN (setting reference)

```
Urastan: a poor market district of a gothic city where everyone has a magical gift.

## 3. MOIMI (age-appearance 7, unchanging)

> No art exists yet — this is the design from the text. **She must be clearly different from Ria** (see note in section 4).

### Who she is (for mood, not to show directly)
The Light that was sealed together with Darkness 1700 years ago and rotted in the dark. Looks like a child, is not a child. Gentle voice, cold curiosity. Not a guardian angel.

### Consistency anchors
```
MOIMI, a girl who looks exactly 7 years old (Dorum's age) and never ages.
Very pale, almost translucent porcelain skin with a faint cold glow.
Long straight hair of pure WHITE-SILVER (no lavender tint), floating weightlessly
around her as if underwater, softly glowing in darkness.
Eyes completely black from edge to edge — no whites, no iris — like a night sky,
with one or two tiny pinpoints of light deep inside.
Small, delicate, perfectly still. Expression: a calm, faint half-smile; curious, not kind.
```

### Clothing
- Simple dirty ash-grey shift dress, hem torn, too thin for winter.
- **Barefoot**, feet never quite dirty from the ground she stands on.
- No accessories, no symbols. Nothing of this world sticks to her.

### Magic look
- Where she steps, floating stones fall and the world "straightens": gravity returns, dust settles.
- Her finger-snap (Ch. 5): a dry click, and darkness recedes "like water sucked back into a drain".
- Her light is **cold white**, never violet — violet belongs to Dorum's power and to Ria.

### Key states for Part I
| Chapter | State |
|---|---|
| Ch. 3 | Not visible — only a pale suggestion of a girl's silhouette in the closet's darkness. |
| Ch. 4 | Standing in the crater among floating stones, hair drifting, black eyes on Dorum. |
| Ch. 5 | Two fingers raised mid-snap; darkness pulling back around her. |
| Ch. 6 | On the dark hill at night, only her glowing hair makes her visible; she points into the dark. |

### Expression panel (6 faces)
1. Calm half-smile. 2. Head tilted, curious. 3. Sad-looking ("something like grief"). 4. Whispering "Too early…". 5. Amused (rare, unsettling). 6. Completely blank.

### Ready prompt
```

Thin torn ash-grey shift dress, barefoot, white-silver hair floating as if underwater,
cold white glow, entirely black eyes without whites. Front / side / back views,

detail close-up of her black star-pricked eyes and of her raised fingers mid-snap.

## 6. KEY SCENES OF CHAPTERS 1–6 (ready illustration prompts)

1. **"The stone that did not fall" (Ch. 1)** — a small black-haired barefoot boy in a muddy alley; a grey cobblestone frozen in the air a hand's width from his face, trembling; a well-fed boy running away in the background. Violet haze around the stone.
2. **"Winter" (Ch. 1)** — two small boys (one black-haired, one ginger) huddled under one torn blanket in a freezing closet, frost on the walls, a single thread of moonlight.

4. **"The stranger" (Ch. 2)** — a cloaked swordsman crouching in a smoky tavern to look into the eyes of a small boy holding a rag; something dark and deep reflected in the boy's eyes.
5. **"The voice" (Ch. 3)** — a child awake in a pitch-black closet, a faint pale silhouette of a girl beside him, barely visible.
6. **"Not his eyes" (Ch. 4)** — a bruised boy slumped against a wall opening fully black eyes; a big cobblestone frozen in the air before him; a crowd of children backing away.
7. **"The crater" (Ch. 4)** — a destroyed square turned into a crater; stones slowly orbiting in the air; children lying in the dust; a white-silver-haired girl with black eyes standing before the kneeling boy.
8. **"Too early" (Ch. 5)** — the girl snapping two fingers; violet-black darkness being sucked back into the boy.
9. **"The father" (Ch. 6)** — a crying bearded city guard with a knife lifted into the air by invisible force; a terrified crowd at the crater's edge.
10. **"The burning tavern" (Ch. 6)** — a limping boy holding his ribs in a narrow alley; behind him a tavern in flames; an old woman motionless on the threshold watching him go.
11. **"Beyond the walls" (Ch. 6)** — a tiny figure on a dark hill outside the city, a starless sky, a faintly glowing white-haired girl pointing into the unknown.
